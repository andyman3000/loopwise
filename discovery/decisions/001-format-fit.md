An opportunity is a need or a pain a customer has. It is never a solution.
A KPI branch is one way the business goal can grow.

## What we decided
We work on C1 for the next eight weeks. C1 is the buyer need "I can't tell which format fits my group." ([discovery/opportunity-comparison.md:43](../opportunity-comparison.md#L43)).
The product lead chose it: "C1 because the evidence is very good and it affects a step very early in the funnel." ([discovery/opportunity-comparison.md:175](../opportunity-comparison.md#L175)).

## Options considered
The product lead confirmed four candidates for the comparison ([discovery/opportunity-comparison.md:117](../opportunity-comparison.md#L117)).
- C1: "I can't tell which format fits my group." ([discovery/opportunity-comparison.md:43](../opportunity-comparison.md#L43)). Chosen.

Buyers fear their people slip back into old habits after the training ([discovery/opportunity-comparison.md:120](../opportunity-comparison.md#L120)). That need sits on Branch B, which is parked this quarter ([discovery/kpi-tree.md:25](../kpi-tree.md#L25)).

## Why, with the evidence it rests on (file and line)
The product lead gives two reasons. The lines below are where the comparison shows each one.

The evidence is very good:
- The comparison rates C1 well supported. 11 buyers say it, in three source groups ([discovery/opportunity-comparison.md:45](../opportunity-comparison.md#L45)).
- A buyer writes: "Side by side, I can't tell which one is us." ([sources/inquiry-mails/inquiry-07.md:16](../../sources/inquiry-mails/inquiry-07.md#L16)).
- A buyer writes: "I didn't want to send your form with a guess in it, so I am writing instead." ([sources/inquiry-mails/inquiry-07.md:18](../../sources/inquiry-mails/inquiry-07.md#L18)).
- A buyer in the inbox writes: "I looked at your formats but cannot tell which one fits" ([sources/inbox/inbox-export.md:125](../../sources/inbox/inbox-export.md#L125)).
- A buyer in an interview says: "One event or three, and in what order, that was the question I brought" ([sources/interviews/sponsor-ines.md:49](../../sources/interviews/sponsor-ines.md#L49)).

It affects a step very early in the funnel:
- The step is the start rate. 546 of 740 inquiry page visits never start the form ([discovery/opportunities/data.md:51](../opportunities/data.md#L51)).
- That step comes before the inquiry is sent. Only the reach rate comes earlier ([discovery/opportunity-comparison.md:32](../opportunity-comparison.md#L32)).
- This quarter's KPI branch is more inquiries that end in a booking ([product/strategy.md:12](../../product/strategy.md#L12)).

Counter-evidence the product lead had in front of them:
- Four buyers knew their format. One writes: "we would like to book an AI workshop for our product and UX team, around 15 people." ([sources/inquiry-mails/inquiry-06.md:12](../../sources/inquiry-mails/inquiry-06.md#L12)).
- The site already says: "The scoping call shapes the real format." ([site/formats.html:21](../../site/formats.html#L21)). Buyers still feel stuck.
- Every sender in the C1 evidence wrote to the academy in the end. Buyers who gave up silently are in no source ([discovery/opportunity-comparison.md:85](../opportunity-comparison.md#L85)).

## We would switch if
The product lead's words: "We would switch if we learn that the real problem is not that people dont know which format to pick, but if they dont book because we cannot convince them enough, that our training is really different" ([discovery/opportunity-comparison.md:176](../opportunity-comparison.md#L176)).
