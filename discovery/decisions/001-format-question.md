## Why, with the evidence it rests on (file and line)
- Six sponsors say it, in three kinds of source. In a mail: "I have budget for training this year but no clear idea what to ask for." (sources/emails/inquiry-03.md:14). In an interview: "I have no idea." (sources/interviews/sponsor-soft.md:49). In the inbox: "I would like to avoid booking the wrong thing." (sources/inbox/inbox-export.md:125).
- The strategy names this pain: "Before booking, sponsors often cannot say which format they need or how they would recognise" (product/strategy.md:26).
- We can change it on the website, before and inside the form. The data shows each of those steps: 546 of 740 inquiry page visits leave without starting the form, and 81 of 194 started forms are not sent (discovery/opportunities/data.md:44, discovery/opportunities/data.md:49).
- My first try was C1. C1 only matters after the scoping call, and "Nothing is logged about what happens in the call." (discovery/opportunities/data.md:61).

## We would switch if
The people who run the scoping calls tell us that sponsors who don't book are worried the training won't last, not unsure about the format. Then we switch to C1.
