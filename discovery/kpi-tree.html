Your first tryFind website, Open website, read stuff, open form, fill form, schedule call, decide on detials, book. I think most people dont even get to the form
Where the tree differs"Read stuff" has no driver of its own. The data shows which pages a visit sees, but no driver counts reading.
"Decide on details" has no event. The data goes straight from call to booking.
Each step is a count or a rate of the step before it.a branch the strategy parks this quarter
Business goalmore booked in-house workshops per quarter
KPI branch Ainquiries that end in a booking
KPI branch B · not this quarterfollow-up bookings
Step 1website visits number of visits to any page of the website Measured: visits with a page_view
Step 2reach rate percentage of website visits that open the inquiry page Measured: visits with a page_view of the inquiry page ÷ all visits
Step 3start rate percentage of inquiry page visits that start the form Measured: inquiry_started ÷ inquiry page visits
Step 4submit rate percentage of started forms that are sent Measured: inquiry_submitted ÷ inquiry_started
Step 5call rate percentage of sent inquiries that get a scoping call Measured: scoping_call_booked ÷ inquiry_submitted
Step 6booking rate percentage of scoping calls that end in a booking Measured: booking_confirmed ÷ scoping_call_booked
bookings = website visits × reach rate × start rate × submit rate × call rate × booking rate
