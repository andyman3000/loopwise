An opportunity is a need or a pain a customer has. It is never a solution.
A KPI branch is one way the business goal can grow (`discovery/kpi-tree.md:6`).
A driver is something people do that can be counted and that moves a branch (`discovery/kpi-tree.md:7`).

## Your first try
"I dont know how much this costs, clear solution: aks for a price on the website" · Where the draft differs: the draft also files the price list as a solution idea. It does not make cost the need. The same mail says "I have budget for training this year but no clear idea what to ask for" (`sources/inquiry-mails/inquiry-03.md:14`). So the draft puts this buyer under O1 ("I can't tell which format fits my group") and O7 ("I don't know what result to expect at the end").

Feedback: The strong part is the label. A price list is a request for a solution, and the answer says so. The weak part is the need. "I don't know how much this costs" repeats the word "price" from the quote and goes no deeper. Two lines further down, the buyer says the budget is there and the open question is what to ask for. A need read from the quote alone missed that line.
