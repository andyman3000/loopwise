## Opportunities

### O1: The sponsor does not know what to ask for, or what they would get
- **Evidence:**
  - "Honestly I am not sure what exactly we need." (sources/emails/inquiry-01.md:14, director digital products, sponsor)
  - "I don't really know what the difference would mean for us." (sources/emails/inquiry-01.md:16, director digital products, sponsor)
  - "I also don't know yet whether we need a workshop, a longer accompaniment or something else." (sources/emails/inquiry-02.md:21, VP product, sponsor)
  - "I have budget for training this year but no clear idea what to ask for." (sources/emails/inquiry-03.md:14, team lead product, sponsor)
  - "I genuinely don't know what makes sense, or what I should even expect at the end of it." (sources/emails/inquiry-03.md:14, team lead product, sponsor)
  - "But I am also not sure what the alternative is." (sources/emails/inquiry-04.md:18, head of product management, sponsor)
- **Voices:** 4 distinct speakers: sponsors (inquiry-01, 02, 03, 04). Second-hand: no.
- **KPI branch:** Branch A: inquiries that end in a booking, driver call rate. [assumption: a sponsor who does not know what to ask for asks for material in writing instead of booking a scoping call.] Two of the four ask for written material first: "Could you send me some information so I can forward it internally?" (sources/emails/inquiry-01.md:18) and "do you have a price list or an overview of your formats you could send me?" (sources/emails/inquiry-03.md:12). The same sender also offers a call: "maybe a 20 minute call would be easier" (sources/emails/inquiry-03.md:16).
- **Counter-evidence in this source:** "we are interested in your hands-on workshop for product owners, about 12 participants." (sources/emails/inquiry-05.md:12) and "we would like to book an AI workshop for our product and UX team, around 15 people." (sources/emails/inquiry-06.md:12): two senders already know the format.
- **Gaps:** The mails are synthetic. They are messages that were sent, so they say nothing about sponsors who gave up before sending. Whether any of the four booked a call is not in this source.
- **Assessment:** well supported. Four of six senders say it in their own words, in different forms. Two counter-examples are clear. The link to call rate is an assumption, with two mails that point to it.

### O2: The sponsor wants proof that it changes how people work, before a call
- **Evidence:**
  - "What I have not seen is evidence that any of it changes how people actually work three months later." (sources/emails/inquiry-02.md:14, VP product, sponsor)
  - "How do you measure whether it worked? What would you show me afterwards?" (sources/emails/inquiry-02.md:19, VP product, sponsor)
  - "before I set up a call I would like to understand a few things." (sources/emails/inquiry-02.md:12, VP product, sponsor)
  - "And how would I know after a quarter that this time it stuck?" (sources/emails/inquiry-04.md:18, head of product management, sponsor)
- **Voices:** 2 distinct speakers: sponsors (inquiry-02, 04). Second-hand: no.
- **KPI branch:** Branch A, driver call rate. The mail ties the call to the answer: "If you can answer these in writing, I am happy to follow up with a call." (sources/emails/inquiry-02.md:23). [assumption: without an answer on proof, this sponsor does not book the call.]
- **Counter-evidence in this source:** "Happy to explain more in a call." (sources/emails/inquiry-04.md:20): the other sender asks the proof question but is ready for a call without an answer first. None of the other four mails asks for proof.
- **Gaps:** Two voices. Neither says which proof would be enough. Synthetic material.
- **Assessment:** partly supported. Two senders, and one makes the call depend on it. The other four do not raise it.
- **Why separate from O3:** O2 is about proof up front, for someone who has not tried yet. O3 is the experience of a training that already failed. inquiry-04 has both.

### O3: A first training did not stick, and the sponsor does not want to repeat it
- **Evidence:**
  - "I am reaching out because our first attempt did not really work." (sources/emails/inquiry-04.md:12, head of product management, sponsor)
  - "But when I asked around a few weeks later, almost nobody was using any of it." (sources/emails/inquiry-04.md:14, head of product management, sponsor)
  - "So I don't want to repeat the same two days." (sources/emails/inquiry-04.md:18, head of product management, sponsor)
- **Voices:** 1 distinct speaker: sponsor (inquiry-04). Second-hand: yes, she reports what her people said: "People said they now know what the tools can do, but not what to do with them in their own work." (sources/emails/inquiry-04.md:14)
- **KPI branch:** Branch A, driver booking rate. [assumption: a sponsor who was let down once needs a sign in the scoping call that this time is different, and does not confirm without it.]
- **Counter-evidence in this source:** none found in this source. No mail reports a training that worked.
- **Gaps:** One voice. Synthetic material. inquiry-02 hints at the same doubt about offers in general (sources/emails/inquiry-02.md:14), but from no own experience, so it is not counted here.
- **Assessment:** partly supported. Vivid and first-hand, with second-hand reports inside it, but one sender. It rests on one voice, so it needs another source to hold.

  - "Some of them already use chat tools, some don't." (sources/emails/inquiry-01.md:14, director digital products, sponsor)
- **Voices:** 2 distinct speakers: sponsors (inquiry-06, 01). Second-hand: no. Only inquiry-06 names it as a worry; inquiry-01 states the mix as a fact.
- **KPI branch:** Branch A, driver booking rate. [assumption: a sponsor with a mixed group checks in the scoping call how the format handles it, and does not confirm if unsure.]
- **Counter-evidence in this source:** "we would like to book an AI workshop" (sources/emails/inquiry-06.md:12): the worried sender already wants to book. The worry is a question, not a stop.
- **Gaps:** One sender names the worry. Synthetic material.
- **Assessment:** partly supported. Clear in one mail, the mix stated in a second. No mail says it would stop a booking.

### O5: The person who inquires must convince someone else, and needs something to pass on
- **Evidence:**
  - "Management wants all product teams to "get up to speed with AI" this year, and I have been asked to sort something out." (sources/emails/inquiry-01.md:14, director digital products, sponsor)
  - "Could you send me some information so I can forward it internally?" (sources/emails/inquiry-01.md:18, director digital products, sponsor)
  - "I will have to take this to my boss, and "the team liked it" will not be enough for him." (sources/emails/inquiry-02.md:21, VP product, sponsor)
- **Voices:** 2 distinct speakers: sponsors (inquiry-01, 02). Second-hand: no.
- **KPI branch:** Branch A, driver booking rate. [assumption: the booking is confirmed by someone who was not in the scoping call, so what the inquirer can pass on decides it.] Could also sit on call rate, since inquiry-01 asks for material instead of a call.
- **Counter-evidence in this source:** "I have budget for training this year" (sources/emails/inquiry-03.md:14): this sender holds the budget and names no one to convince.
- **Gaps:** Two voices. The mails do not say who decides in the end, or what they would need to see.
- **Assessment:** partly supported. Two senders say it plainly; one other clearly decides alone.
- **Why separate from O2:** O2 is what the sponsor wants to know for themselves. O5 is what they need to hand to someone above them. inquiry-02 has both in the same line.
