> Mixed material: the free-text answers are paraphrased from an anonymised composite of two clients. All ratings are invented ([sources/feedback/feedback-export.md:3](../../sources/feedback/feedback-export.md#L3)). Every finding below repeats that label.

An opportunity is a customer need or pain the team could address. It is never a solution.
A KPI branch is one way the business goal can grow.
A driver is something people do that you can count, and that moves a branch.
Counter-evidence is a line in the same source that speaks against the opportunity.
