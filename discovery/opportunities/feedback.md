## Opportunities

### O1: Participants know what they could do with AI, not yet what they will do in their own work
- **Evidence:**
  - "Now I have an idea of what I could do with it, but not yet of what I will do with it." (sources/feedback/feedback-export.md:37, R01, participant)
  - "so I could not really place yet where this fits." (sources/feedback/feedback-export.md:38, R02, participant)
  - "I would like a direction for how to use this day to day, not only in the exercises." (sources/feedback/feedback-export.md:40, R03, participant)
  - "Show us how to apply this, or part of it, to features that are actually on our roadmap." (sources/feedback/feedback-export.md:41, R04, participant)
- **Voices:** 4 distinct respondents: participants. Second-hand: no.
- **KPI branch:** Branch B: follow-up bookings, not this quarter. [assumption: what participants can say they use afterwards is what the sponsor hears, and that decides whether more is booked.] No Branch A driver: participants do not inquire or book.
- **Counter-evidence in this source:** "That one I will keep using." (sources/feedback/feedback-export.md:46, R09). "I connected it to our ticket tool to break concepts down into tickets faster." (sources/feedback/feedback-export.md:47, R10). "I can see myself sitting down with another department and building a quick version of what they ask for." (sources/feedback/feedback-export.md:48, R11).
- **Gaps:** Written at the end of day 2, so it says nothing about the weeks after. Q2 asks exactly this (sources/feedback/feedback-export.md:15), but its ratings are invented.
- **Assessment:** partly supported. Four respondents say it in their own words, and three others clearly found their use. The only link is to Branch B, through an assumption.
- **Why separate from O2:** O1 is not seeing where it fits. O2 is losing what was learned once the normal week starts, even when the fit is clear.

### O2: Without a plan for the weeks after, the training fades into the normal week
- **Evidence:**
  - "Methods from trainings are the first thing to go when the pressure is on" (sources/feedback/feedback-export.md:38, R02, participant)
  - "What I missed: a plan for the next weeks." (sources/feedback/feedback-export.md:42, R05, participant)
  - "What are the next steps and what results are we expected to get?" (sources/feedback/feedback-export.md:42, R05, participant)
  - "Assigned next steps, and a way to improve the process bit by bit after the training." (sources/feedback/feedback-export.md:43, R06, participant)
  - "it would help to meet again once I have made some progress." (sources/feedback/feedback-export.md:44, R07, participant)
  - "After most workshops I think it was nice and then carry on exactly as before." (sources/feedback/feedback-export.md:45, R08, participant)
- **Voices:** 5 distinct respondents: participants (R02, R05, R06, R07, R08). Second-hand: no.
- **KPI branch:** Branch B: follow-up bookings, not this quarter. [assumption: a team that keeps using it after the training is what makes a sponsor book again.] No Branch A driver.
- **Counter-evidence in this source:** "This time I have a good feeling I will build it into my daily work." (sources/feedback/feedback-export.md:45, R08): the same respondent expects it to stick this time. "I will get a few things started at work with this." (sources/feedback/feedback-export.md:49, R12).
- **Gaps:** Nobody here reports the weeks after; these are fears and wishes on day 2. R05 and R06 phrase it as a wish for a plan, which is a solution; the pain behind it is the fade R02 and R08 name.
- **Assessment:** partly supported. Five respondents point at it, two name the fade in their own words. Only a Branch B link, through an assumption.

### O3: Participants with little background get lost early in the day
- **Evidence:**
  - "A bit lost at the start: no schedule and no concrete goal for the day." (sources/feedback/feedback-export.md:52, R15, participant)
  - "I walked in with no background at all and thought it would be far too much." (sources/feedback/feedback-export.md:54, R17, participant)
  - "I fell behind on day 1" (sources/feedback/feedback-export.md:46, R09, participant)
  - "The persona exercise lost me after a handful of questions, I closed it." (sources/feedback/feedback-export.md:55, R18, participant)
  - "Watching someone's screen and doing my own work at the same time did not work for me remotely." (sources/feedback/feedback-export.md:53, R16, participant)
  - "I came in not knowing what to expect" (sources/feedback/feedback-export.md:37, R01, participant)
- **Voices:** 6 distinct respondents: participants. Second-hand: no.
- **KPI branch:** Branch B: follow-up bookings, not this quarter. [assumption: a group where some get lost reports back less use to the sponsor.] No Branch A driver. The moment sits after the booking, during preparation and the day itself.
- **Counter-evidence in this source:** "Being guided one to one on the second day is what saved it for me." (sources/feedback/feedback-export.md:54, R17). "Quick pace, but okay." (sources/feedback/feedback-export.md:56, R19). R09 and R18 both caught up later in their own answers (sources/feedback/feedback-export.md:46, sources/feedback/feedback-export.md:55).
- **Gaps:** The day itself is training content, which this repo does not own. Only preparation before it (expectations, setup) is in scope. Q3 asks about pace, but its ratings are invented.
- **Assessment:** partly supported. Six respondents describe being lost or behind, several recovered. The link to a branch is only through Branch B.

### O4: The setup is not ready when the training starts
- **Evidence:**
  - "I installed the code editor for the first time the day before." (sources/feedback/feedback-export.md:51, R14, participant)
  - "I had to request more memory for my laptop before the setup would run." (sources/feedback/feedback-export.md:58, R21, participant)
  - "The click prototype needed a separate login first, and the forms in our local HTML files did not submit in the browser." (sources/feedback/feedback-export.md:60, R06, participant)
  - "Sharing files through the company drive was slow, a repository would have been faster." (sources/feedback/feedback-export.md:59, R03, participant)
- **Voices:** 4 distinct respondents: participants. Second-hand: no.
- **KPI branch:** Branch B: follow-up bookings, not this quarter. [assumption: time lost on setup lowers what participants report back.] No Branch A driver. The setup check belongs to sponsor preparation on the website, which the tree does not measure.
- **Counter-evidence in this source:** "It worked well enough for one day." (sources/feedback/feedback-export.md:59, R03): the same respondent calls the friction minor. No other respondent mentions setup.
- **Gaps:** Q4 asks exactly this (sources/feedback/feedback-export.md:17), but its ratings are invented. The answers do not say how much time was lost.
- **Assessment:** partly supported. Four concrete, first-hand cases. One calls it minor. No Branch A link, and the moment is after booking.
