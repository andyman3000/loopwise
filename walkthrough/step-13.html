We believe that

"Buyers who cannot tell which format fits will answer a few questions about their team. They then trust the format the quiz names enough to start the form."

We go on if

At least 3 of 5 testers answer the quiz for their own group, take the step from the result into the form, and send it with the named format still in it.

We rework or drop if

The line has two parts. A problem with the page means rework. A problem with trust in the named format means drop. We rework the quiz if 3 or more of 5 testers get stuck on a question, or cannot find the way from the result to the form. We drop the idea if 3 or more of 5 testers delete the format the quiz named, or reach the form and leave without sending it. If the five testers fit none of these lines, the result is mixed. The learning card then decides on another round.
