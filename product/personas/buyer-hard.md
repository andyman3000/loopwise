> "Whether they use what they learned in a normal week, and whether they show the person next to them." (`interviews/sponsor-thomas.md:89`)

## Context

- The first step shrank from months to one prep day and about a day and a half in the room (`interviews/sponsor-mehmet.md:37`).
- Pressure from above: management expects a big jump in efficiency right after the workshop, says the AI programme lead (`interviews/sponsor-thomas.md:101`). At the top of another company the expectation is "much faster, soon" (`interviews/sponsor-mehmet.md:73`).
- Most people are open to AI and "just don't know how to make it part of their day" (`interviews/sponsor-thomas.md:117`).
- The group ranges from people who built a fair amount themselves to a big middle who only touch the built-in features (`interviews/sponsor-thomas.md:121`).
- Product owners were not part of the agent training the developers did (`interviews/sponsor-mehmet.md:89`).
- Access gaps: the company platform cannot write into the ticket system and has no link to the roadmap tool (`interviews/sponsor-thomas.md:133`).
- One company-wide platform is the standard; what gets built should be set up there (`interviews/sponsor-thomas.md:109`, `:113`).

## Goals

- Show the effect, then decide on more: a smaller first step first (`interviews/sponsor-mehmet.md:37`).
- One or two assistants or skills set up that people keep using afterwards (`interviews/sponsor-thomas.md:109`).
- Behaviour change, not awareness: people use it in a normal week and show the person next to them (`interviews/sponsor-thomas.md:89`).
- Names a number before the workshop: cycle time (`interviews/sponsor-thomas.md:89`).
- Follow-up afterwards: short check-ins, then one longer session some weeks later (`interviews/sponsor-mehmet.md:177`).

## Frustrations

- **Trainings that changed nothing.** His head of engineering has seen people "slide back into their old habits" (`interviews/sponsor-mehmet.md:173`).
- **Wrong kind of format.** Two days on a narrow list of use cases instead of the whole job (`interviews/sponsor-thomas.md:93`); one level that cannot suit everybody (`interviews/sponsor-thomas.md:121`).
- **Tools that do not connect.** "The platform can't write into the ticket system" (`interviews/sponsor-thomas.md:133`).
- **Sprawl.** "I won't hand everyone every tool just because it exists." (`interviews/sponsor-thomas.md:129`)

## How they decide

- Wants to speak to a comparable reference before a call (`inquiry-mails/inquiry-02.md:18`).
- Has to take it to the boss (`inquiry-mails/inquiry-02.md:21`, `interviews/sponsor-mehmet.md:29`).
- Has no measure in place: "We don't really have product KPIs anyone steers by." (`interviews/sponsor-mehmet.md:93`)
- Next step depends on how the first one goes (`interviews/sponsor-mehmet.md:37`).

## What they say

- "I'll pay for a tool that shows it earns its keep." (`interviews/sponsor-thomas.md:129`)
- "Two good days, we'll manage. What worries me is the Monday after" (`interviews/sponsor-mehmet.md:169`)
- "Good question, and I don't have a clean answer." (`interviews/sponsor-thomas.md:81`)
- "One thing I won't do is go out and promise a number" (`interviews/sponsor-mehmet.md:77`)

## Where the same pattern shows up

- Asks for references and how success is measured before a call; "the team liked it" will not be enough for the boss (`inquiry-mails/inquiry-02.md:18`, `:19`, `:21`).
- A first training did not stick; "how would I know after a quarter that this time it stuck?" (`inquiry-mails/inquiry-04.md:14`, `:18`).
- Also unsure of the format: workshop, longer accompaniment or something else (`inquiry-mails/inquiry-02.md:21`, `inquiry-mails/inquiry-04.md:18`).

## What the hard buyer is NOT

Not a participant. Not hostile: approved a first step and is fine with small gains (`interviews/sponsor-mehmet.md:37`, `interviews/sponsor-thomas.md:85`). Not someone with a product measure in place (`interviews/sponsor-mehmet.md:93`), though one names cycle time (`interviews/sponsor-thomas.md:89`).

## What we do not know yet

- Which measure would satisfy this buyer and the boss. A head of engineering asks which metrics would show the new process is used (`interviews/sponsor-mehmet.md:173`); no source answers it.
- Whether access problems delay or prevent a booking. Not in the material.
- Budget size. Not in the material, and we keep prices out of it.
