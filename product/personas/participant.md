# Persona: the participant

> "By definition we all have the same job title. What people actually do day to day is quite different." (`interviews/participants-group-1.md:59`)

**Persona type:** primary user of the format, not the buyer
**Basis:** three anonymised composite group interviews (13 participants), one feedback export with paraphrased free text (ratings invented, not used here), synthetic inbox entries. Traces are relative to `sources/`.

## Mixed level, same title

In the three group interviews, two of 13 participants say they already run agents
(`interviews/participants-group-2.md:17`, `interviews/participants-group-3.md:15`), three describe
themselves as beginners or not advanced (`interviews/participants-group-2.md:15`,
`interviews/participants-group-2.md:51`, `interviews/participants-group-3.md:13`). Sponsors see the
same spread (`interviews/sponsor-hard.md:45`, `emails/inquiry-06.md:14`).

## Goals

- Practical examples to carry straight into the day, not knowledge only (`interviews/participants-group-1.md:47`).
- Apply it to features actually on the roadmap (`interviews/participants-group-2.md:43`, `feedback/feedback-export.md:41`).
- A plan for the next weeks with next steps (`interviews/participants-group-2.md:43`, `feedback/feedback-export.md:42`).
- Someone who sits with them on their own topic (`interviews/participants-group-3.md:59`).
- Material to reread afterwards (`interviews/participants-group-2.md:57`, `feedback/feedback-export.md:56`).
- A shared way of working and sharing skills in the team (`interviews/participants-group-2.md:55`, `interviews/participants-group-3.md:63`).

## Frustrations

- **Back to normal the next morning** (`interviews/participants-group-1.md:35`, `feedback/feedback-export.md:45`).
- **Does not know where it helps.** "It's honestly a hunch. There has to be more." (`interviews/participants-group-1.md:25`, `feedback/feedback-export.md:37`)
- **Format built for someone else:** written for developers, prepared prompts (`interviews/participants-group-1.md:33`); tools the company does not allow (`interviews/participants-group-2.md:25`).
- **Lost at the start:** no schedule, no goal for the day (`interviews/participants-group-2.md:47`, `feedback/feedback-export.md:52`); following a screen remotely while working (`interviews/participants-group-2.md:21`, `feedback/feedback-export.md:53`).
- **Setup friction:** laptop memory (`interviews/participants-group-2.md:29`, `feedback/feedback-export.md:58`), no admin rights (`inbox/inbox-export.md:77`).
- **Time** to find out where it went wrong (`interviews/participants-group-3.md:23`).

## Counter-evidence to keep in view

- Access is not a blocker for several: `interviews/participants-group-1.md:19`, `interviews/participants-group-3.md:47`, `inbox/inbox-export.md:65`.
- Some do take it into their work: `interviews/participants-group-1.md:39`, `interviews/participants-group-3.md:25`, `feedback/feedback-export.md:46`.

## How they decide

They do not book. Their own test is whether they open the assistant for the next task
(`interviews/participants-group-1.md:41`, `feedback/feedback-export.md:45`). What they tell the
sponsor afterwards is what the sponsor hears (`interviews/sponsor-soft.md:25`).

## What they say

- "On the day everything is great, and then the next morning you carry on exactly the way you did before." (`interviews/participants-group-1.md:35`)
- "I'll have an idea of what I could do with it. Not: this is what I'm going to do with it." (`interviews/participants-group-2.md:13`)
- "Honestly, someone who sits with me on my own topic." (`interviews/participants-group-3.md:59`)

## What the participant is NOT

Not the buyer (`interviews/sponsor-soft.md:71`, `interviews/sponsor-hard.md:13`). Not one level. Not a developer by default (`interviews/sponsor-soft.md:17`).

## What we do not know yet

- Whether bringing their own task changes what they report four weeks later. No follow-up data yet.
- The level spread in a typical booked group. The interviews give 13 self-descriptions, not a distribution.
