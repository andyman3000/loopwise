# Vision

Status: draft. Grounded in the material under `sources/` (synthetic files and one feedback export with
paraphrased free text).

## Who we serve

Product teams in companies: product managers, product owners, UX and consultants with product
duties, booked in-house by a buyer who wants "something with AI" for the team
(`sources/inquiry-mails/inquiry-03.md:14`). Two people matter in every booking: the buyer who decides, and
the participants who have to make it work in their own week.

## The problem we exist for

Trainings end and nothing changes. A buyer puts it as "Feedback on the days themselves was fine.
But when I asked around a few weeks later, almost nobody was using any of it" (`sources/inquiry-mails/inquiry-04.md:14`).
Participants know what the tools can do, "but not what to do with them in their own work"
(`sources/inquiry-mails/inquiry-04.md:14`). And before booking, buyers often cannot say which format they
need (`sources/interviews/sponsor-ines.md:49`, `sources/interviews/sponsor-carla.md:141`) or how they
would recognise success (`sources/inquiry-mails/inquiry-03.md:14`, `sources/inquiry-mails/inquiry-04.md:18`).

## What we believe

- **The buyer names the success line before booking**, the way a product team names it before
  building. A format without a success line is a guess, for the buyer and for us.
- Participants work on their own real task, not on prepared exercises
  (`sources/interviews/sponsor-thomas.md:177`, `sources/interviews/participant-francoise.md:193`).
- The work after the format counts as much as the days themselves
  (`sources/interviews/sponsor-mehmet.md:169`).

## What we are not

- Not a tool course. Where the tools will be in a year or two, nobody knows
  (`sources/interviews/sponsor-mehmet.md:189`).
- Not a talk or a screen to copy from: participants do the work themselves
  (`sources/interviews/sponsor-ines.md:149`, `sources/interviews/participant-francoise.md:177`).
- Not an open seminar: we work in-house, for one team.
- Not a price list: the format and the success line come first.

## What success looks like in a few years

Buyers come to the scoping call with a success line already written. Four weeks after a format,
participants use what they learned in a normal week. A buyer names this as the sign he will
watch (`sources/interviews/sponsor-thomas.md:89`). Other teams in the same company ask for the format
on their own. [assumption: no source reports this yet] Many of our workshops come from customers who
