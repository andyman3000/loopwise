# Vision

Status: draft. Grounded in the material under `sources/` (anonymised composites and synthetic files).

## Who we serve

Product teams in companies: product managers, product owners, UX and consultants with product
duties, booked in-house by a sponsor who wants "something with AI" for the team
(`sources/emails/inquiry-03.md:14`). Two people matter in every booking: the sponsor who decides, and
the participants who have to make it work in their own week.

## The problem we exist for

Trainings end and nothing changes. A sponsor puts it as "Booked, everyone thought it was nice, four
weeks later everything was as before" (`sources/interviews/sponsor-hard.md:23`). Participants know
what the tools can do, "but I have no idea how to use any of it sensibly in my actual work"
(`sources/interviews/sponsor-soft.md:21`). And before booking, sponsors often cannot say which
format they need or how they would recognise success (`sources/interviews/sponsor-soft.md:49`,
`sources/interviews/sponsor-hard.md:37`).

## What we believe

- **The sponsor names the success line before booking**, the way a product team names it before
  building. A format without a success line is a guess, for the sponsor and for us.
- Participants work on their own real task, not on prepared exercises
  (`sources/interviews/participants-group-1.md:33`, `sources/interviews/participants-group-3.md:41`).
- The work after the format counts as much as the days themselves
  (`sources/interviews/sponsor-hard.md:29`).

## What we are not

- Not a tool course whose clicks are outdated in six months (`sources/interviews/sponsor-hard.md:41`).
- Not a talk with agents built on stage (`sources/interviews/sponsor-soft.md:37`).
- Not an open seminar: we work in-house, for one team.
- Not a price list: the format and the success line come first.

## What success looks like in a few years

Sponsors come to the scoping call with a success line already written. Four weeks after a format,
participants can name one thing they do differently (`sources/interviews/participants-group-1.md:51`).
Other teams in the same company ask for the format on their own
(`sources/interviews/sponsor-soft.md:61`), and most of our format days come from customers who have
booked before. [assumption: follow-up bookings become the larger share; not counted yet]
