# Participant group interview 3

- **Basis:** real conversations, anonymised composite
- **Format:** group interview, excerpt
- **Date:** Autumn
- **Participants:** P1, analyst in an internal service team · P2, product manager · P3, product manager on the business side · P4, product specialist, day-to-day operations and projects · P5, product owner
- **Interviewer:** I

---

**I:** I'd like to hear about your own topics. When you work with the assistant, what is it working on?

**P1:** I'm in a lot of spreadsheets right now, planning and controlling. With numbers I really like the assistant. I've built myself a check or two. But I'd still call myself very much a beginner.

**P2:** I'm further along, I think. I already work with agents. But I'm still the one connecting them. What I want is enough know-how to build pipelines that run without me in the loop, so I can spend the time on the roadmap and on stakeholders.

**I:** P1, what would you want to do next?

**P1:** I tried to set up a project. But what's missing is someone walking alongside me on the topics that actually sit with me. I don't even know what the idea could turn into. And I imagine there's a lot more possible. I just wouldn't know how to build an app or a clickable page. For that I really need someone who can.

**I:** What stopped you from trying it on your own?

**P1:** Time, mostly. Blocking out a whole afternoon just to find out where I went wrong, that hasn't happened.

**P5:** I had no big idea what to expect either. On the first day I got left behind a bit with the persona part. But then I built myself a small interface for one of my own cases, and on the second day we developed it further. That's something I'll stick with. I'll use it, and it gives me good results.

**I:** So you worked on something from your own job.

**P5:** Yes. We have a real case where we need to deliver data, so we'll come back to that for sure.

**I:** P3, you had a training recently.

**P3:** Yes, a basic part and an advanced part. That untangled something for me. But skills are a huge lever that I'm not using yet. I'd need one that first loads the background of my product and the people involved, and only then starts on the task.

**I:** Can you give me an example from last week?

**P3:** I have to prepare a presentation for next week, on a topic I've explained more times than I can count. And I didn't do it with AI. Because I haven't automated it so far, I do it by hand.

**I:** What would have to change?

**P3:** I think you'd have to show each person in their own context. Train people individually. What matters in your work? What skill do you need? With the right skill, I think everyone would use it and not just understand it in theory. Then it would really pay off.

**P4:** For us it's the sharing that catches. Everyone works with their own assistant. Sure, you can create skills and share them, but that's where it still gets stuck. Everyone researches on their own, has things explained to them, and then pulling that back together is hard.

**I:** Is access part of that?

**P4:** Not really. I have the wiki and our day-to-day tool connected. The product context sits in a file we share.

**P1:** I tried connecting the ticket tool. It didn't work in a way I liked. I let it fill things in and nothing useful came out.

**I:** P3, how does that look around you?

**P3:** Many people still use AI very selectively. Here's a mail, summarise this mail, without any context at all. A few colleagues have filled their context properly, and when I saw that I thought: I want that too. I think that's the biggest lever.

**P2:** Going in, what I wanted was to go deeper than where I am now. A workflow that runs without me coordinating it by hand.

**I:** If you picture the next few weeks, what would help?

**P1:** Honestly, someone who sits with me on my own topic. Working on the real thing together would teach me more than another course.

**P5:** For me it's clear. I'll keep building on the thing I started.

**P4:** A way to share the skills we create that actually works between us.

**I:** Thank you all.
