# Feedback export: closing form, two-day in-house training

Basis: mixed. Free-text answers are paraphrased from real feedback (anonymised composite of two clients); all numbers and ratings are invented for the demo.

- Format: Loopwise in-house training for a product team, two days
- Form: anonymous, sent at the end of day 2
- Responses: 21 of 24 participants (invented)
- Export: raw, as downloaded from the form tool. Not cleaned, not grouped.

## Form questions

| ID | Question | Type |
|---|---|---|
| Q1 | How would you rate the training overall? | Scale 1 (poor) to 5 (excellent) |
| Q2 | I know how to use what I learned in my own work. | Scale 1 (disagree) to 5 (agree) |
| Q3 | How was the pace? | Scale 1 (far too slow), 3 (about right), 5 (far too fast) |
| Q4 | My setup was ready before the first session started. | Scale 1 (disagree) to 5 (agree) |
| Q5 | How likely are you to recommend this training to a colleague? | Scale 1 (unlikely) to 5 (very likely) |
| Q6 | What worked well for you? | Free text, optional |
| Q7 | What should we change? | Free text, optional |
| Q8 | How will you use this in your work? | Free text, optional |
