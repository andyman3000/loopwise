Sponsor preparation

Four steps between the scoping call and the day itself, and one afterwards.

1. Participant list

Send us the names, roles and email addresses of the participants at least two weeks before the format. Tell us who is a manager of whom, and who joins remotely.

2. Setup check

One week before the format we check the room or video tool, network access, accounts for the tools we will use, and any security rules that restrict them. Please name a contact person on your side.

3. Expectations survey

Each participant gets a short survey before the format: what they hope to take away, which questions they bring, and what they already know. We share the summary with you.

4. Follow-up after four weeks

Four weeks after the format we ask participants and the sponsor what has changed in daily work, and we agree on possible next steps.
