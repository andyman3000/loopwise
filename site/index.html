Loopwise

In-house formats for product teams: product managers, product owners, UX designers and consultants with product duties.

We run formats inside your company, from a half-day workshop to several weeks of team coaching. Topics include discovery, prioritisation and Agentic Product Management.

How it works

  1. You send an inquiry.
  2. We clarify the format in a scoping call.
  3. We prepare together: participant list, setup check, expectations survey. Four weeks after the format we follow up.
