Half-day workshop

Duration: 4 hours, on site or remote.

Audience: product managers and product owners who want one topic in depth, up to 16 people.

Two-day training

Duration: 2 consecutive days.

Audience: a product team of 8 to 20 people, mixed roles including UX.

Agentic Product Management lab

Duration: 2 days plus a 2-hour follow-up session.

Audience: product managers who want to work with AI agents on real tasks from their own backlog.

Multi-week team coaching

Duration: 6 to 12 weeks, one session per week.

Audience: one intact product team working on a live product.

Shadowing

Duration: 3 to 5 days inside the team, followed by a feedback session.

Audience: individual product managers and leads who want feedback on how they work day to day.
