What is concretely different four weeks later, and how do we measure it?

We settle this on the scoping call. For every format, we pick two or three signs you can read in your team's work four weeks later: a shipped change, a conversation you would not have had before, a decision made with different inputs.

How would I know after a quarter that this time it stuck?

We set a short follow-up with you around week four. One call, one page: what carried over, what did not. If nothing stuck, we say so.
