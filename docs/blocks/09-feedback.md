# Block 9: Feedback and your results

**What it's for:** the trainer sees what your pair built and what you thought of the two days, and
improves the workshop with it.

## What you do (about 15 min)

1. Your branch started before this skill existed. Fetch it, inside the `loopwise` folder:
   ```bash
   git fetch origin
   git checkout origin/build -- .claude/skills/workshop-feedback docs/blocks/09-feedback.md
   ```
   Restart Claude Code, so it sees the new skill.
2. In the fresh session after the restart, run `/workshop-feedback`. Each of you answers five short questions on your own. Hand
   over the keyboard when the skill asks.
3. The skill shows the files it will pack: your artefacts in `discovery/`, your variant in
   `site/variants/`, your git history and your answers. Say yes, or name what to leave out.
4. The skill asks separately whether to add your Claude Code transcripts. They show the trainer
   where the skills confused you. They also hold everything you typed. Say yes only if nothing
   private or secret is in them.
5. The skill saves one zip on your Desktop and shows it. **Mail it yourselves** to
   mail@andreasstephan.com, subject `Loopwise feedback <your branch>`. The skill sends nothing.

**Done when:** the mail with the zip is sent.

The answers carry your name, because you send the mail. Skip any question you do not want to
answer.

**Next:** nothing. This is the last block of the workshop.
